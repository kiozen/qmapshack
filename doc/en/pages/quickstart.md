# Quick start

The quick start takes you from the first start of QMapShack to a track on your GPS device. The images on all pages
show the same area, the Tannheimer Tal in Austria. Read them in order: each page builds on the one before.

```{toctree}
:maxdepth: 1
:hidden:

quickstart-main-window
quickstart-maps
quickstart-vector-map
quickstart-zoom-pan
quickstart-dem
```

- [The main window](quickstart-main-window.md): the panels around the map and how to arrange them; the home folder for your data.
- [Turning on maps](quickstart-maps.md): the built-in online maps, stacking them and making one see-through.
- [Turning on a Garmin map](quickstart-vector-map.md): Garmin vector maps, their level of detail and their look.
- [Zooming and panning](quickstart-zoom-pan.md): moving around the map and the zoom range a map is shown in.
- [Elevation data and hillshading](quickstart-dem.md): adding elevation data (a DEM), the elevation under the mouse
    pointer and a shaded terrain.
